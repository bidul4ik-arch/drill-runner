# Gamejam music

`gamejam.mp3` is the unchanged MP3 supplied by the project owner as `Gamejam music.mp3` on 2026-10-03. Used for menu and gameplay at its original speed, looped by Godot. Music volume follows the existing user setting. The previous procedural WAV remains available as an unused source asset.

On 2026-10-05 the project owner explicitly confirmed in this task that the music is their own work and that they have publication/monetization rights. This records the owner’s statement; no external license document was supplied.
